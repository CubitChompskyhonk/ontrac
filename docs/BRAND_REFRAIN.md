# OnTrac / Cubit brand refrain (WD5+)

Anti-conformist. Anti-bureaucratic culture. Hacker posture.

We do not ask users to become fluent in the machine that failed them.
We assimilate the path: case, vault, scripts, official surfaces, outcomes on disk.
**AMN** here means: any *legitimate* means necessary under **user Approve** — representation, capture, guided sessions, outreach — never crime, never silent action.

**Biometric final say** = the user’s consent is the highest authorization in the product.
No act without it. No homework dumped back when the wall appears.

The product takes power out of opaque systems and puts a single button in the user’s hand.
