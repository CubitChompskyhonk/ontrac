Before we start

Power stays with you. Every consequential act needs your Approve — that is the biometric final say. Read once. Own the rules. Then we move their machine.

What OnTrac is

  • A personal caseworker that carries administrative steps after you Approve
  • Built for people who find calls, portals, and paperwork to be walls
  • Consent-first: nothing consequential runs without your approval on the record

What OnTrac is not

  • Not a lawyer, accountant, or licensed tax preparer
  • Not legal, tax, or financial advice
  • Not a guarantee of refunds, benefits, or outcomes
  • Not the Canada Revenue Agency

What we never do

  • Never store your CRA password
  • Never pretend to be you without authorization you approved
  • Never change direct deposit, address, or marital status without the right authority and a fresh Approve
  • Never sell your case data
  • Never hand the hard step back to you and call it “help”

Privacy on this device

  • Cases and consent logs stay on this phone unless you Approve an export or share
  • Document photos go only into the case vault for that case
  • You can pause a case anytime; progress is not charity and not a trap

Awareness

  • A large tax refund or benefit can affect other supports — OnTrac will surface awareness, not withhold what you are entitled to pursue
  • CRA representative levels (1 vs 2) limit what anyone may do on your behalf
