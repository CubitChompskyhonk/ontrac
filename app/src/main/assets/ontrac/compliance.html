Rules of engagement

Priya’s pass: plain language on authority, limits, and awareness. Required before pilot.

Representative levels (CRA)

  • Level 1 — mainly disclosure of information
  • Level 2 — certain account changes (address, direct deposit, etc.)
  • Legal representative / POA is a separate path with formal documents
  • OnTrac will not claim powers you have not authorized through official channels

Awareness — large refunds & benefits

  • Recovering years can produce a large refund or credit
  • A large payment can affect other income-tested supports
  • OnTrac will not hide money you are entitled to pursue; we surface the awareness so you decide with eyes open

What we will not do

  • Store CRA passwords
  • Guarantee refund amounts or outcomes
  • Act as your lawyer or licensed tax preparer unless that path is explicitly engaged later
  • Expand scope past a specific Approve
